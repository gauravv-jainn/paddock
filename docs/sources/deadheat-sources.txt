https://fixedoddshorseracinguk.com/articles/dead-heat-rules-betting-payouts/
================================================================================
Two-horse dead heat:
"Consider a £10 win bet at 6/1 in a two-horse dead heat. Your £10 stake becomes
two £5 portions. One £5 wins at 6/1, returning £35 (£30 profit plus £5 stake).
The other £5 is lost. Your total return is £35 from a £10 investment - £25
profit rather than the £60 profit you'd have received from an outright win."

Three-way dead heat:
"That same £10 at 6/1 becomes three £3.33 portions. Only one-third wins:
£3.33 x 7.00 = £23.31. From your £10 stake, you receive £23.31 back - a modest
profit rather than the life-changing win you might have anticipated."

Place dead heat (no figures given):
"If you backed a horse at 3/1 for a place and it dead-heats for third with one
other horse, half your stake wins at 3/1 and half loses."

WARNING ON THE THREE-WAY EXAMPLE: £23.31 is reached by rounding the divided
stake to £3.33 BEFORE multiplying by the odds. Dividing exactly
(10/3 x 7.00 = 23.333...) gives £23.33. `.claude/rules/money.md` requires
rounding once, at the end, which yields £23.33 and NOT the source's £23.31.
This example is carried in published.json flagged `disputed: true` and MUST NOT
be treated as a passing target until a bookmaker source settles the question.
