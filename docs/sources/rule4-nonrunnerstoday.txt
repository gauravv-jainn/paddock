## Worked Example

"If you staked £10 on a horse at 6/1 and the Rule 4 deduction is 25p, your
gross profit was £60. The deduction is £60 x 0.25 = £15. Your adjusted profit
is £45, and your total return is £55 (£45 plus your £10 stake)."
