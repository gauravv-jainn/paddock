## Worked Examples (Verbatim)

Example 1: "You place a £20 win bet on Horse A at 4/1. Before the race, Horse B
- the 6/4 joint-favourite - is withdrawn...a 40p deduction...winnings would be
£80...£80 x 0.40 = £32. Your adjusted winnings are £80 minus £32, which equals
£48. Add your £20 stake back, and the total return is £68 instead of £100."

Example 2: "Same bet - £20 on Horse A at 4/1 - but this time the withdrawn
horse was a 12/1 outsider...5p...Your winnings are still £80. The deduction:
£80 x 0.05 = £4. Adjusted winnings: £76. Total return with stake: £96."

Example 3: "Two horses are withdrawn...Horse B was priced at 3/1 (25p
deduction) and Horse C at 5/1 (15p deduction...deductions stack: 25p + 15p =
40p...£80 x 0.40 = £32. Adjusted winnings: £48. Total return: £68."
