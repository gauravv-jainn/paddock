NOTES

1. ROW 15: this source publishes "100/30" (3.333 fractional) where four others
   publish "16/5" (3.2). Both are in sequence between 3/1 and 9/2, so unlike
   racing-index's 16/15 this is not self-evidently wrong — it is a genuine
   two-way disagreement about the band's lower bound, at 4 sources to 1.

2. The worked example states TOTAL RETURNS, so it is usable as a golden vector.
   Both figures are internally consistent with the six-source table:
     £20 at 5/1, 3/1 withdrawn (row 14 = 25p):
         winnings £100, less 25% = £75, plus £20 stake = £95   as stated
     plus 10/1 withdrawn (row 18 = 5p), cumulative 30p:
         winnings £100, less 30% = £70, plus £20 stake = £90   as stated
   Carried as pub-r4-008 and pub-r4-009 in tests/golden/published.json.
