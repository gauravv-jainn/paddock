## Worked Example

The guide provides this calculation example: "a 5p rule 4 deduction on a £100
stake on a 10/1 winner will reduce your profit by 5%. The profit on this bet
would ordinarily be £1,000 so 5% of that is £50 - that's the deduction. The
total return on the bet would be £1,050."
